Why it matters

Kazakhstan's next chapter is being written in code

Kazakhstan is investing heavily in its digital future — from smart city services in Astana and Almaty to AI in agriculture across the steppe. But technology only transforms a country when its people understand it.

AI literacy isn't just for programmers. It's for teachers choosing classroom tools, farmers monitoring livestock, doctors reaching rural patients, and elders staying safe online. Everyone has a stake.

What AI literacy gives you

  • Confidence to use AI tools at work, school, and home
  • Ability to spot AI scams, deepfakes, and misinformation
  • New career opportunities in a growing IT sector
  • A voice in how AI is used in Kazakh society

Practical applications

AI is already working across Kazakhstan

Real uses, close to home. Pick a sector to see how.

AI on the steppe

Satellite imagery and machine learning help farmers monitor crop health across vast fields, predict yields, and track livestock with smart sensors — critical in a country where agriculture spans millions of hectares.

Try it: upload a photo of a plant leaf to an AI plant-disease app and get a diagnosis in seconds — a tool already used by agronomists in Kostanay region.

Bridging Qazaq, Russian, and English

AI translation is breaking language barriers — and helping preserve and grow the Kazakh language online, from speech recognition for Qazaq to AI tools that generate learning materials in Latin script.

Try it: dictate a message in Kazakh into a translation app and send it in English to a colleague abroad — instantly.

Smarter Astana and Almaty

AI powers traffic management, public service chatbots on eGov, and energy optimization in Kazakhstan's cities — making daily life faster and government services more accessible.

Try it: use the eGov mobile assistant to renew documents without visiting a service center.

Healthcare that reaches the regions

AI-assisted diagnostics and telemedicine connect rural patients with specialists in major cities, and help radiologists read scans faster and more accurately.

Try it: telehealth platforms let a patient in a remote village consult an Almaty specialist the same day.

AI for the corner shop and the startup

From writing product descriptions to forecasting inventory and answering customer messages automatically — AI tools give small businesses in every region big-company capabilities.

Try it: ask an AI assistant to draft your bazaar stall's Instagram posts for the week in both Kazakh and Russian.
